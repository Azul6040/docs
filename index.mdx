Restaurantero Pro is the financial intelligence layer for restaurants. Your POS sends daily transaction data — sales, payments, cash register cuts — and Restaurantero Pro transforms it into actionable insights: P&L statements, food cost percentages, cash reconciliation, menu engineering matrices, and AI-powered alerts. This documentation guides POS developers through authentication, data ingestion, and consuming intelligence endpoints.

## How it works

Restaurantero Pro sits on top of your POS. You push raw transaction data to the ingestion endpoints after each cash register cut, and then pull processed intelligence back to display in your POS dashboard or manager app.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Get your API key">
    Log in to the Restaurantero Pro dashboard, go to **Settings → Integrations → API Keys**, and generate a key for your branch.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send your first Corte Z">
    After a day-end cash register cut, POST orders, payments, sales, and the cash-register cut to the ingestion endpoints in the correct order.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Consume intelligence">
    Query the intelligence endpoints to retrieve P&L, KPIs, food cost, alerts, and menu engineering data for display in your POS or manager dashboard.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set up webhooks">
    Register a webhook URL to receive real-time notifications when snapshots complete, alerts trigger, or cash variances are detected.
  </Step>
</Steps>
